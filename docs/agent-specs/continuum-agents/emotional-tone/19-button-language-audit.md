# Agent 19: Button & CTA Language Audit
**Priority:** P1 - HIGH
**Estimated Time:** 4 hours
**Dependencies:** None
**Category:** Emotional Tone

---

## OBJECTIVE

Audit and replace all button text and calls-to-action to use compassionate, non-demanding language.

**Current Issues:**
- Imperative verbs ("Save", "Delete", "Submit")
- Demanding tone
- No emotional consideration
- Technical language
- Task completion focus

**Expected Outcome:**
- Gentle, supportive button language
- User agency emphasized
- Compassionate confirmations
- Contextappropriate tone
- Non-demanding CTAs

---

## LANGUAGE TRANSFORMATIONS

### Save/Submit Buttons:
- ❌ "Save" → ✅ "Keep this safe"
- ❌ "Submit" → ✅ "Save my thoughts"
- ❌ "Update" → ✅ "Update when ready"

### Delete/Remove Buttons:
- ❌ "Delete" → ✅ "Remove this"
- ❌ "Remove" → ✅ "I'd like to remove this"
- ❌ "Cancel" → ✅ "Not right now"

### Navigation:
- ❌ "Next" → ✅ "Continue when ready"
- ❌ "Back" → ✅ "Go back"
- ❌ "Skip" → ✅ "I'll do this later"

### Confirmation Dialogs:
- ❌ "Are you sure?" → ✅ "Just checking - remove this?"
- ❌ "Confirm deletion" → ✅ "Yes, remove it"
- ❌ "OK" → ✅ "I understand"

---

## IMPLEMENTATION

Replace button text throughout codebase with compassionate alternatives.
