## Checked and fine
- Zero console errors, page errors or 4xx/5xx on all 63 signed-in routes in both themes, and on all admin routes (as admin). Signed out, the only failures are the expected 401/404 for bogus tokens on /auth/verify, /for, /portal, /pulse/portal, /pulse/respond and /pulse/verify, each rendering a calm "link has rested / closed for now" page.
- No horizontal overflow at 1440x900 on any route in either theme (`scrollWidth <= clientWidth` everywhere).
- No endless spinners. The only `animate-*` elements still present after 8s are decorative (marketing globe hover, pulse glow, grief heart).
- Overlap heuristic hits are by design: sidebar items scrolling under the fixed "Your plan" footer, and the send button inside the drafting-assistant input. No clipped text.
- Signed out, protected routes (/dashboard, /modules/*, /onboarding, /catalog, /help, /resources/grief-support, /welcome) redirect to /login. Non-admins on /admin/* go to /dashboard.
- Failed-save state, forced 500 on create: pets, contacts, calendar, subscriptions, legacy journal, timeline, medical and financial accounts all keep the modal open and show "Everything you've saved is safe / Your draft is kept… / Try Again / Close for now". Nothing persists after reload. Try Again after recovery saves exactly one record (pets checked through the API).
- Failed delete (forced 500): calendar, property, subscriptions and heirlooms keep the item and show "It is still in your list… Try Again".
- Non-SyncManager saves under a forced 500: pulse vault ("your words are still here"), executor-toolkit sharing choice ("ready to save again") and admin automation config ("Your draft is kept") all show a failure state.
- The primary "Add/Record" buttons on 12 modules open their forms. A button-effect probe on dashboard (header and main), simulator, scenario-mode, analytics, /executor, /help, digital-guardian, home-manual, qr-codes and treasure-hunt found no dead buttons.
- No speed talk in rendered text or source strings (instant, quick, fast, seconds, immediately). The one "Real-time" is finding 13.
- Dashboard counts match the seeded data (2 contacts, 1 account, 7 "Kept safe"), and the status-chip filter works.
